Macro Risk

The one bet underneath all eight

These are not eight independent positions. They are roughly one AI-capex bet made eight ways. If any of the three pillars below cracks, most of the list falls together.

01

Hyperscaler capex keeps climbing

2026 combined hyperscaler capex guidance sits above ~$500B. Every name here — compute, memory, neocloud, power, storage — lives or dies on that number. A capex cut hits all eight simultaneously. This is the single most important variable to track.

02

Memory cycle doesn't roll over

MU, SNDK, and AMD's margins need tight supply. A NAND/DRAM oversupply move from Samsung or SK Hynix would break the memory trade. Samsung's quarterly capex announcements are the leading indicator — watch them.

03

Risk appetite and financing stay open

IREN, CIFR, NBIS, FLNC, and BE are all capital-hungry and several are leveraged. A credit tightening, rate spike, or risk-off rotation compresses the whole high-beta complex simultaneously — even if the AI capex story stays intact.
